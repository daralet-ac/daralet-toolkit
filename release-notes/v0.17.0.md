## Quest Tracker

- **A floating Quest Tracker.** Turn it on in the Quests tab's Settings. It lists the quests you've started, each on one line with its progress ("3 / 10") and time left.
- **Click a quest to open its details:** its stage and timer, a progress bar, the description, and who gives it, who you hand it in to and the quest area, each with **Go** to point the World tab's arrow there. The tracker remembers which quests you left open.
- **Not started too** also lists quests you've been given but haven't started. **Open all** and **Close all** open or fold every quest at once.
- **Hide quests from the tracker.** Untick a quest in the new **Track** column of the Quests tab, or right-click it in the tracker and choose **Hide from tracker**. **Show hidden** in the tracker's settings brings them all back.
- With **Only when needed** on, the tracker hides itself when there's nothing to track.

## Text size and layout

- **The Text size now reaches everything.** The Quests list, the inventory lists, loot rules, market search, leaderboards and other lists stayed small whatever the slider said. They now follow it.
- **Table columns grow with the text,** so bigger text is no longer cut off.
- **Drag the dividers between panes** to resize them: the Loot tab's rule list, the Inventory's pack list, the Hotbars page's list of bars, the Leaderboards' list of boards, and the Quests list above its details. Double-click a divider for its usual size. Sizes are saved and grow with the Text size.
- **The resize cursor stays on screen.** Hovering a window's edge or corner, a divider or a text box used to show its cursor for only a moment. It now stays while the mouse is there, which also helps other plugins' windows.

## Target windows

- **Choose which vitals show.** The Target and Tracked Target windows can show or hide Health, Stamina and Mana one by one, put them **Side by side**, and set a **Bar width**, as the fellowship window can.
- **The same options are in each window's right-click menu.** Right-click anywhere on the window to open it.

## Fixes

- **Fellowship buttons no longer crash the game.** Leader, Dismiss, Open, Recruit, Quit and Disband in the Social tab, and Invite in the friends list's right-click menu, crashed the client. They now send the same messages as the game's own fellowship panel.
- **The looter no longer says "is your main pack full?" for items it looted.** An item that landed after the corpse closed, or after another corpse opened, was reported as a failure. The looter now counts an item as looted once it's in your packs.
- **The looter uses your side packs.** Every item went to the main pack, so a full main pack stopped all looting even with room in your side packs. Items now go to the first pack with room. With no room anywhere, the looter stops and says so, and when it does give up on an item it says the likely reason.
- **Locked sigil slots are hidden.** The inventory's paperdoll showed all three sigil slots for every character. It now shows only the slots you've unlocked.
