## Escape closes windows

- **Press Escape to close the plugin's windows.** The `/dap` window and the Inventory, Bank, Character and Quests windows
  all close at once. Closing the Bank window this way closes the bank, as its X does. Target, Fellowship, Loot, hotbars,
  the map and the other windows that follow what you're doing stay open.
- **Escape still does its usual job first.** While you're typing in chat or a text box, have a menu open, are holding a
  tool, or are picking a hotbar key, Escape only does that and closes nothing.
- **It's on to start.** Switch it off in the Config tab's new **Escape key** section.

## Splitting stacks

- **A number typed in the game's stack box now counts** when you drag from the plugin. Before, the drag carried the
  slider's last amount, often the whole stack, unless you clicked somewhere in the game first. The Split dialog's
  **Drag them** takes the typed number too.
