## New

- **Right-click to appraise.** Right-click an item, creature, NPC or player to appraise it straight away. This works in your inventory, the bank, the paperdoll, the Corpse and Vendor windows, hotbar slots, the Radar, the fellowship, the Dungeon map's markers and the Target windows (and the names in a threat table). It works out in the game world too: a quick right-click on a monster, NPC, player or object appraises it, while holding the right button still turns the camera. To get an item's or creature's right-click menu, hold **Alt** while you right-click. You can pick another key, or turn this off to get the old menus back, under **Right-click** in the Config tab's Text section.
- **Copy loot rules to another profile.** Right-click a loot rule or a folder and pick **Copy to profile** to copy it into another of your loot profiles. It goes at the end of that profile's list, in a folder of the same name. This is the easy way to take a rule from the example profile into your own.
- **Pyreals in loot rules.** Misc's **Type** has a new **Pyreals** choice for loot rules that are just for coins. The example profiles' Pyreals rule now uses it. Rules you made before that pick up coins as Other still work.
- **Wield a weapon in your off hand.** Drop a one-handed melee weapon on the paperdoll's Shield square to wield it in your off hand. Dropping something you're wearing on another square it fits moves it there, such as a sword from one hand to the other.

## Changes

- **Appraise opens the game's own panel.** With **Plugin appraisal** off, Appraise now opens the game's appraisal panel right away, instead of only selecting the item for your appraise key.
