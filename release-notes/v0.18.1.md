## Shared window

- **Attach to toolbar follows the toolbar.** The shared window now sits right above the toolbar and as wide, wherever
  you move it, and below it when there's no room above. It used to stay where the game's panels last were.
- **It takes turns with the game's panels both ways.** A game panel opening still closes the shared window, and now
  opening one of the shared windows closes the game's panel, so the two no longer sit on top of each other.

## Hide the game's hotbar

- **Hide hotbar now makes the toolbar shorter.** Before, the slots went but the toolbar stayed just as big, so there was
  nothing to see for it. Now it shrinks to its buttons, keeping its bottom edge in place (its top edge, if it's in the
  top half of the screen). It goes back when you put something on the bar, turn the setting off or log out, and your
  toolbar's saved place isn't changed.

## Quests

- **The Quests list fits narrow windows.** Its columns are as wide as what's in them, and Progress and Time are left out
  while no listed quest has one. In the attached shared window, the Quest column used to shrink to a letter and Track
  was pushed off the edge.
