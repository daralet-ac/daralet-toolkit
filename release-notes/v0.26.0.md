## New

- **Link Status window.** Replaces the game's Link Status panel, opened from the link indicator, and can be one of the shared window's windows. It times your round trip to the server with the game's own ping, once a second, and shows the last ping and, over the last 1, 5, 15 or 60 minutes, your usual ping, the lowest, the highest, jitter, spikes and lost pings. A graph shows each ping, with spikes in red, and the newest spikes are listed underneath. With **Keep measuring** on, it carries on timing once opened, even with the window closed.
- **Book window.** Books, notes and anything else with writing open in a plugin window drawn in the game's own art: the parchment, the curled corners to turn the page, and the gold page menu. A new book opens at its first page, and one you've read before opens where you left it. The text has its own size, sharp at any size, set in the window's settings or with Bigger and Smaller in its right-click menu. It can be one of the shared window's windows. To write in a book, pick **Write in the game's window** from the right-click menu.
- **Style menu on items.** An item's right-click menu (in your inventory lists and on hotbar slots) has a **Style** submenu, the server's /style for just that item. It lists the looks you can switch to, with the one showing ticked and those earned from quests starred. Pick one to switch to it. Unlike /style, it always changes the item you picked, even if you've appraised something else since.
