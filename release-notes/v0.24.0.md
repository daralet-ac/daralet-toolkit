## Radar

- **A new Radar window takes the place of the game's radar.** Tick **Radar** in the Config tab's **Client UI Override** to hide the game's and show the plugin's. Its settings are in the World tab's **Settings**, and in its right-click menu.
- **Round or square, any size.** Drag its edges to resize it; it settles square when you let go. Round with the **Retail** look, it's the game's own gold-rimmed radar and compass letters, upscaled so they stay smooth at large sizes.
- **Zoom with the mouse wheel,** in as close as 5 meters and out as far as the game's own radar reaches: 75 meters outdoors, 25 indoors. Indoor and outdoor zoom are kept apart.
- **Every kind of blip, your way.** Monsters, NPCs, players, player killers, PK Lite players, admins, fellows, portals, lifestones, corpses, doors and chests: show or hide each kind and pick its colour. Anything the server gives a colour of its own, like a portal device's purple, shows in that colour, as on the game's radar.
- **Click a blip to select it,** and hover for its name and distance. Your selection is ringed in its own colour, or in white if you'd rather.
- **The dungeon map underneath.** Indoors, the map of the dungeon you're in is drawn under the blips. Set how solid it is, how many floors below and above yours it shows, and whether the rooms you've been in are marked. Blips on other floors are drawn faint.
- **Turn with me, compass letters, coordinates and range rings,** each on or off.

## Loot rules

- **Name has takes alternatives.** Separate them with commas, and any one will do: "iron, steel sword" matches anything iron, or a steel sword. Before, a comma counted as part of the word, so such a rule matched nothing and items fell through to the rules below it. A catch-all Salvage rule lower down could then salvage everything.
- **Salvage rules can pick their materials.** A Salvage rule has a **Material** list under Types: tick any number to salvage only items made of one of them. With nothing ticked it takes every item, as before.

## Good to know

- **The Radar starts off.** The game's radar stays until you tick **Radar**'s Replace box.
