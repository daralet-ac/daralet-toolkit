## XP bar

- **A new XP bar** shows how far you are through your level, in a window of its own to keep on screen. Turn it on under the Character tab's Settings, in the **XP bar** section. The Character tab also shows it now, at the top of your stats, with every detail written out.
- **It moves the moment a kill lands,** and hovering it shows everything: your level, the XP into it and to the next, XP/hour, and your rested experience.
- **Rested experience** you've built up is shaded in blue ahead of your XP, showing how far its bonus would carry you. A line under the bar says whether you're resting, or why not (not in a town, in combat mode, below level 10), and how much is left.
- **XP/hour.** Turn it on to see your XP an hour over the last 5, 15, 30 or 60 minutes or since login, and how long your next level will take at that rate. Right-click the window to start it again.
- **Compact** shows only the bar, with no text on it or lines under it. Hover it for the details. It's in the settings and the window's right-click menu.
- **Make it yours:** width, height, what's written on the bar (numbers, percent, both or none), your level, short or full numbers, dividers, and the colours of the fill, the rested shading and the background.

## Rested experience

- **Build up bonus XP while you rest.** From level 10, resting in a town out of combat mode builds up rested experience, and logging out builds it at half that rate. While you have some, your kills earn double experience until it's used up. A fellow's share of a kill counts too.
- **It builds faster at lower levels,** about five kills' worth an hour in town at any level, and slows the more you've banked. It stops building at level 50 for now, but what you've banked still spends.
- **Type `/rested`** to see how much you have, whether you're resting and how fast it builds, in town and logged out. When you log in, a line says how much you rested while away.

## Inventory

- **See what's on your hotbars.** Items on a hotbar you have showing get the game's shortcut mark in the Inventory window and tab: the icon's top left corner in pale gold, with the key that uses it. Untick **Hotbar highlights**, beside Loot highlights, to turn it off.
