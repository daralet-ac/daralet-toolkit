## The game's panels, the plugin's way

- **Replace the game's own panels with the plugin's windows.** Each has a **Replace** box: tick it and the game's panel stays hidden while the plugin's window takes its place. Every one comes in a **Retail** look, drawn with the game's own art, or **Modern**, in the plugin's theme. The boxes are in the Config tab's **Client UI Override**, and the Vitals, Power bar and Spell bar settings are on the Combat tab.
- **Vitals, Power bar and Spell bar.** Your health, stamina and mana; the power and accuracy bar with High, Medium and Low; and the game's eight tabs of favourite spells, which the game's own keys still work on.
- **Toolbar and Indicators.** The combat button, the panel buttons, the selection strip with Use and Appraise, and the indicators beside it.
- **Attributes,** laid out as the game's pages: raise attributes, vitals and skills with your unassigned XP, and train skills with your skill credits (the green arrow, dulled when you haven't enough).
- **Titles** has a page of its own in the Attributes window: see every title you have and pick the one other players see.
- **Spellbook** with its components and the **Buy to** levels the game's fill-components uses. Right-click a spell and pick **Appraise** for its school, mana, duration, its range at your skill, and its components.
- **Options** (the Gameplay menu, Character options and Config), **Buffs**, **Debuffs** and **Vitae**.
- **Vendor:** the game's three tabs, the kinds menu, full item tooltips on the vendor's stock, **Loot highlights** on its slots, and the Selling tab opening as you drag something over it.
- **Creature Appraisal:** a creature's or player's appraisal as the game shows it, its sex, heritage, template and player killer status included, with the creature's **3D model** behind its stats.

## Appraisals

- **Appraise from the toolbar opens the plugin's appraisal** for whatever you have selected: an item, a creature, an NPC or a player.
- **One appraisal window.** Item, creature and spell appraisals share one window, so a new one replaces the last instead of piling up. Each has a close button in its top right corner.

## Windows

- **No more title bars.** Move a window by dragging its top edge, as the game's panels move, with the game's own move cursor. Hold **Ctrl** to drag any window from anywhere on it, locked or not.
- **Resize nearly any window by its edges.** The XP bar, Vitals, Power bar and Spell bar get wider or narrower from their left and right sides. Windows that size themselves grow or shrink as you drag an edge. A locked window resizes with **Ctrl** held.
- **Snapping as you go.** Windows snap to the screen's edges, to each other and to the grid while you move or resize them, not only when you let go. The grid has thicker lines at the middle of the screen and its thirds and quarters, and a line lights up gold when a window is centred on it. **Snap resizing to the grid** is a setting of its own, on to start. Hold **Shift** to place a window freely.
- **Map:** while it shares the shared window, **Pop out** takes it into a window of its own in the middle of the screen, and **Dungeon map** shows the map of the dungeon you're in above the world map.

## Look and feel

- **Drop-down menus, scroll bars and sliders** now use the game's own art.
- **Open sections are easier to follow:** what's under an expanded header is set in, with a line down its left side.

## Tooltips

- **Tooltips now show only while you hold Alt,** so they no longer pop up over everything as you move the mouse. Pick another key, or turn it off to have them always show, in the Config tab's **Text** section, under **Tooltips**.

## Fixes

- **Borderless window** now fills the whole screen, with no strip left at the top.
- **Vendor:** the amount to buy goes back to 1 when you pick another item or open another vendor.

## Good to know

- **The game panels start off.** Nothing of the game's changes until you tick a panel's **Replace** box.
- **Full tooltips on a vendor's stock need the matching server update.** Until then they show what the game already knows: value, burden and stack.
