## Text size

- **Make the plugin's text bigger or smaller.** A new **Text size** slider on the Config tab (50% to 250%, with Reset)
  scales the main window and every floating window. A window's own Size still counts on top of it. The new size applies
  once you let go of the slider, and it's kept in your settings profile.
- **Tooltips follow it too,** at the size of the window they're shown from.

## Splitting stacks

- **Drag part of a stack.** Click a stack in the plugin's inventory to select it, set the game's stack bar, and a drag
  carries that many, shown on the drag icon. Let go over a pack, an empty slot, another item, the toolbar backpack, the
  ground or someone, and only that many go there. Onto a stack of the same thing, they join it.
- **Split... in the right-click menu** of any stack of more than one opens a slider under the mouse. **Split** makes that
  many a new stack at the end of the same pack, and **Drag them** sets the game's stack bar for your next drag. A click
  outside cancels.
- **The new stack lands where you let it go,** not next to the old one or at the top of the pack.

## The game's shortcut bar

- **Drag items from the plugin onto the game's shortcut bar.** Let go over one of its 18 slots and the item goes there,
  as it would from the game's own pack: whatever was in that slot moves to the first empty slot to its right. The slot
  shows the green ring while you hold an item over it. Before, the drop used the shortcut already there and put the item
  on the ground.

## Inventory

- **Deposit and Keep tags.** The pack Tags drop-down now starts with what the pack is for: **Deposit** packs are emptied
  by `/bank deposit packs` (or Deposit with the box empty) and never filled by withdrawals; **Keep** packs are left alone
  by deposits of a kind and by `/sort`.
- **Big stacks show their count.** From 10,000 a stack shows in thousands, like 50.1k. A stack of 50,000 pyreals used to
  show no count at all.
- **Double-clicking quickly down a pack** now puts on every item you click, as the game's pack does, not only the first.
- **Boots only show in the Feet square** of the Worn paperdoll. With no pants on, they used to show as pants too, and
  they can no longer be dropped on the Pants square.

## Item icons

- **Items keep their own white parts.** An item's outline is still coloured by its effect, but white inside the icon is
  no longer turned black.
