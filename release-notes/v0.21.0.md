## Appraisal

- **See what's in each socket.** Item details in the Appraisal window, item tooltips and the Corpse window now list each jewel socket the way the game's appraisal does: "Socket: Black Garnet (5)" for a jewel, or "Empty Jewel Socket". Market search shows them too.
- **Property descriptions on hover.** In the Appraisal window, hover over a property (Biting Strike, Crushing Blow, Mighty Thews and the rest) to read the game appraisal's description of it, roll range included. Any the window has no line for are listed under Description.
- **Gear shows its Use text and description** in the Appraisal window, as items you can't wear already did. Item tooltips stay as short as before.

## Dungeon map

- **Map opacity.** The Dungeon window's settings (World tab, Settings, Dungeon window) have a new **Map opacity** slider that fades the map itself: its dark ground, floors and walls. Markers, your arrow and the way out stay solid. With Background and Map opacity both at 0%, only the map's lines and markers are left over the game.

## Combat text

- **Stamina and mana heals look different.** Floating combat text now shows stamina restored in yellow and mana restored in blue, apart from health in green. Each has its own row in Combat Text, with its own Floating, Log, Save, Hide in chat and colour, so you can recolour or hide each on its own. They start out set as your healing was.
- **Health heals are labelled "Health healing"** in the Combat Text table, the combat log and saved log files.

## Fixes

- **Dropping an item on the ground no longer gives it away.** Dragging an item from the plugin's windows to an empty spot in the world could give it to whatever you had selected before, such as a chicken in town, instead of dropping it. It now only goes to something under the mouse when you let go, and otherwise lands on the ground.

## Good to know

- **Socket details need the matching server update.** Until then, items show only how many sockets they have, on the workmanship line.
