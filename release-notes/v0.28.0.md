## New

- **Salvage window.** Using an Ust opens the plugin's own salvage window, laid out like the game's. Drag items onto it from your inventory windows or the game's packs, or drag a whole pack (the main pack too) to add everything in it that can be salvaged. A green ring or red circle shows whether an item can go on, and retained, unsalvageable and marked-for-sale items are kept off. You can mix materials: Salvage handles them all at once. Click an item to take it off.
- **Add by rules and Add all packs.** **Add by rules** adds every item you carry that your loot profile says to Salvage. **Add all packs** adds everything salvageable except what's in packs tagged Keep.
- **Salvage summary.** Under the slots the window shows the units you'll get by material and tier, what you already carry of each, and the value. It also says how many new bags are needed against your free pack slots, and warns about a missing Ust, items your loot profile keeps and items marked for sale.
- **Salvage results.** After salvaging, a **Last salvage** section lists what you got by material and tier, your new totals and each item, and one line in chat sums it up. Turn on **Hide salvage lines** to keep the server's salvage lines out of chat.
- **Replace the game's salvage panel.** Under the Config tab's Client UI Override, **Salvage panel** keeps the game's salvage panel out of sight while the plugin's window is up.
- **Salvage hammer loot mark.** Loot rules have a new salvage hammer icon. The example profiles' Salvage rule uses it, orange with no border (white in the colorless profile).
- **Stack bar like the game's.** In the Toolbar window, a stack you carry shows the game's number box, which you can type an amount into (set on Enter or when you click away), beside the game's slider. The name shows the amount in front, as in the game ("3 Brass Salvage").
- **Health meter like the game's.** A selected creature's or player's health in the Toolbar window uses the game's own thin bar with the heart, right under the name.
- **The game's own font.** The Toolbar window's selection name and stack number are drawn in the game's own font, matching the client's letters exactly.

## Changes

- **Materials in names.** Selected items show their material in front of the name, in the Toolbar window and your inventory lists, as the game does.
- **One appraisal at a time.** Every appraisal now goes to one place: the Appraisal window (with Plugin appraisal on), Creature Appraisal (while it replaces the game's), or the game's panel. Two appraisals no longer show on top of each other.
- **Creature Appraisal shows more.** Lifestones, portals, doors and other objects now show in Creature Appraisal too, with their name, kind, use text and description.

## Fixes

- A right-click in the game world to appraise no longer makes your character take a step forward.
- Salvage bags no longer turn black when you hover them.
- A salvage bag refined to a higher tier now shows its new workmanship straight away, instead of only after the plugin restarted.
- A salvage bag's workmanship now shows the bag's own, not the total of everything salvaged into it.
- The "You carry" amounts for salvage now show your real totals.
