## Combat text

- **Floating combat text.** Your damage, what's done to you and your healing can rise as numbers on screen. Turn it on
  in the Combat tab's new **Combat Text** tab, where **Preview** shows sample numbers while you set their size, time and
  place. Criticals, kills and deaths show bigger.
- **Over their head** puts the numbers over whoever they landed on instead, centred and stacking upward. They draw on
  top of everything, so a wall won't hide them. It's off to start. The **Height** slider sets where they begin.
- **Choose what goes where.** Each kind of combat line has its own settings: show it as floating text, list it in the
  log, save it to a file, or keep it out of chat. Each has its own colour too.

## Combat log

- **The Combat log window** lists recent combat, each kind in its colour, with a search box. Turn it on in the Combat
  Text tab.
- **Copy a line.** Hover over a line for a **Copy** button. The line turns green when it's copied.
- **Save combat to files.** **Save to files** writes combat to a text file for each character and day, in the `Combat
  Logs` folder. It's off to start, and nothing deletes the files, so they're yours to clear out.

## Borderless window

- **Borderless window** (Config tab, under Game window) takes the title bar and border off the game's window. A window
  as big as its monitor fills it exactly. With no title bar to drag, **Centre** and **Top left** move it. The border
  comes back when you turn it off.

## Item icons

- **Item outlines match the game's.** Icons now use the game's own colour gradients for an item's effect instead of one
  flat colour, and a magical item's outline shows as magical even when it also has an element.
- **Weapons have their own tiles.** Melee weapons and casters now sit on the game's tiles for them, rather than all
  using the missile weapons' one. Market listings show the right tile too.

## Good to know

- **Numbers over creatures with the same name are a best guess.** Chat only gives a name, so the number goes on the one
  you hit last, then your selection, then the nearest. When a cleave hits several at once, the numbers spread across the
  nearest of that name, but which number lands on which creature may not be exact.
- **Creature height isn't known,** so numbers start at about a person's height plus the **Height** slider, and very tall
  or short creatures may look a little off.
