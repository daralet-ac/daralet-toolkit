## Social

- **The Fellowship tab is now Social.** It has five sub tabs: Allegiance, Fellowship (what was Members), Find a group, Friends and Squelch. The Social window shows the same tabs on their own.
- **Allegiance.** See your rank and followers, your patron and monarch with the XP you've passed up, and your vassals with what each has given you. **Swear** swears to your selection. **Break** and **Kick** ask before they do anything.
- **Friends.** Add a friend by name or the player you have selected, see who's online and offline, and switch **Appear Offline**. Right-click a friend to Invite them, Send a tell or Remove them.
- **Squelch.** Squelch a character or a whole account by name. See everyone you've squelched, and remove anyone from the list.
- **Create Fellowship** has a name box and the four fellowship options.
- **The fellowship buttons** Leader, Dismiss, Open, Recruit, Quit and Disband are greyed out when you can't use them, and their tooltip says why.
- **A Social button over the game's toolbar.** Turn it on in the Config tab's Client UI Override. It opens the Social window instead of the game's panel. Enable all and Disable all now cover seven buttons.

## The fellowship window

- **Side by side** puts each fellow's bars in one row, and **Bar width** sets how wide they are.
- **More by each name.** Each of these has its own checkbox: the fellow's level, how far away they are, the Combat Focus they wear, and their buffs and debuffs as icons. Buffs are only the ones that wear off, so item cantrips and auras don't fill the line. Debuffs are framed in red.
- **Level and distance are easier to read.** They were too faded.
- **Right-clicking a fellow opens the menu again,** with their bars and the new Pin to top.

## Big fellowships

- **Three layouts.** Town Defense fellowships have no size limit, so the window's settings have a Big fellowships section. **List** stops growing and scrolls after so many members, 10 to start. **Pages** shows tabs of so many members each, and a tab turns red while someone on it is below half health. **Grid** gives each fellow a small tile, with a health bar and thin stamina and mana strips.
- **Choose who shows.** **Sort** orders members by the fellowship's own order, by name, lowest health first or nearest first. **Show up to** caps how many are shown. **Hide far away** leaves out members too far away to say how far. Right-click someone and choose **Pin to top** to always show them first. The window says how many members it isn't showing.
- **Lighter on the server.** Buffs and debuffs are only asked for in fellowships up to a size you choose, 12 to start. A fellowship of more than nine is asked about every 2 seconds instead of every second.

## Vital bars

- **Retail bars** draws health, stamina and mana bars with the game's own vitals panel art. Find it in the fellowship window's or the Target tab's settings.
- **Bold numbers** makes the text on the bars stand out.

## Hotbars

- **Hotbars stay where you put them.** Logging out and back in used to move them all to the middle of the screen. Now a bar only moves when you drag it.
- **Export and import layouts.** Under the hotbar list, **Export layout** saves every bar's layout, look, keys and place to a file. Tick **With my items** to save your items too. **Import layout** puts a saved layout in place of your bars. Files go in `Documents\Decal Plugins\Daralet Toolkit\Hotbar Layouts`.

## Other

- **A Toolkit button and key.** The Config tab can show a small floating button that opens and closes the `/dap` window. You can put it anywhere and make it any size. You can also choose a key that does the same.
- **Item details text size.** The Loot tab's settings can make item tooltips and the Appraisal window bigger or smaller than the rest of the plugin.

Social, the fellowship window's Combat Focus and spells, and big-fellowship sizes need the matching server update.
